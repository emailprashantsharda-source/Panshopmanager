Collect Essential Customer Information

To verify customers before renting a vehicle, start by collecting essential information. This helps you create a rental profile for each customer.

  • Full Name: Request the customer's full name. This is important for record-keeping and communication.
  • Contact Number: Ask for a contact number. This allows you to reach them if there are any issues.
  • Address: Collect the customer's address. This information can be useful for verification and future reference.

By gathering this information, you create a solid foundation for verifying customers and ensuring a smooth rental process. Always remind your staff to handle this information with care, as it is sensitive data.

Establish a Verification Process

Establishing a verification process is vital for ensuring that you rent vehicles to trustworthy customers. Start by creating a checklist for your staff to follow. This checklist should include key customer details like name, contact information, and driving licence number. Having a structured approach helps staff remember all necessary steps and reduces mistakes.

Training your staff is equally important. They should learn to recognize valid driving licences and understand the different formats used across India. Each state may have its own design, so make sure your staff can identify these variations. Consider holding regular training sessions to keep everyone updated on the latest formats and security features.

Conduct a Vehicle Inspection

Before handing over the vehicle, it is important to conduct a thorough inspection with the customer present. This helps to establish a clear understanding of the vehicle's condition and avoids disputes later. Start by checking for any existing damages such as scratches, dents, or broken parts. Document these damages with notes and photographs. This way, both you and the customer are aware of the vehicle's state before the rental begins.

Next, record the odometer reading and fuel level at the time of pickup. Make sure both parties agree on these numbers. This ensures transparency and sets clear expectations for the return.

Using a checklist can be very helpful during the inspection process. Include items such as:

  • Exterior condition (body, tires, lights)
  • Interior condition (seats, dashboard, cleanliness)
  • Functionality of essential features (horn, wipers, air conditioning)
  • Safety equipment (spare tire, jack, first aid kit)

By following these steps, you can ensure that the vehicle is in good condition and that both you and your customer are on the same page. This can prevent misunderstandings and ensure a smooth rental experience.

Communicate Rental Terms Clearly

Clear communication of rental terms is essential for a smooth rental experience. Start by providing a written rental agreement to your customers. This document should outline all important terms, including:

  • Rental Duration: Specify the start and end dates of the rental period.
  • Fuel Policy: Explain whether the vehicle should be returned with a full tank or at the same level as when rented.

What to Check on the Driving Licence

The driving licence is the one document you cannot skip. Ask for the original card or the digital version, never a photocopy, and check these points with the customer standing in front of you:

  • Photo: The face on the licence should match the person at your counter. If you are unsure, ask them to remove a mask, helmet or sunglasses.
  • Name: The name should match the other ID the customer shows you.
  • Expiry date: Check that the licence is valid for the whole rental period, not just today.
  • Vehicle class: The licence lists the classes the holder may drive. A car needs a light motor vehicle (LMV) entry and a geared motorcycle needs a motorcycle-with-gear entry. A customer with only a gearless two-wheeler entry should not be given a geared bike.
  • Condition: Be careful with a torn, laminated-over or smudged card where the number or expiry cannot be read clearly.

Licence layouts differ from state to state, and older paper and booklet licences are still in use. This is why staff training matters: a card that looks unfamiliar is not necessarily fake, but it deserves a second check.

Verify the Licence Digitally

A printed card is easy to copy, so ask the customer to also show the licence on their phone, in DigiLocker or the mParivahan app. Reports on the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways procedure say that electronic driving licences shown in these apps are accepted as equal to the original by traffic officers, and a 2021 Delhi transport department notice said the same. That makes a digital licence a reasonable standard for your own counter too.

Rules and apps change, so treat this as a practical check and not as legal advice. If the app does not load, ask for the physical card instead of skipping the check.

Ask for a Second ID and a Local Contact

A licence proves that someone can drive. It does not tell you how to find them if the vehicle does not come back. Many shops therefore also collect:

  • A second photo ID, such as a voter ID or passport, to cross-check the name
  • A working mobile number, which you can confirm by calling it while the customer is present
  • A local address or, for visitors, the hotel or place they are staying in
  • A second contact number, for example a family member or colleague

Tourists and out-of-town customers carry the highest risk. Ask for their stay details and a reachable contact. Foreign visitors normally carry an International Driving Permit together with their home-country licence, so ask to see both.

Be Careful With Aadhaar Copies

Many shop owners ask for an Aadhaar photocopy by habit. UIDAI has advised people not to share photocopies of Aadhaar and to use a masked version that shows only the last four digits. It has also said that private organisations without a UIDAI user licence should not collect or keep copies of Aadhaar. Unless you have checked your position with a CA or lawyer, a driving licence plus another ID is the safer route. If a customer offers a masked Aadhaar, you can look at it without photocopying or storing it.

Also avoid holding the customer's original driving licence as security. They need it to drive legally, and an original in your drawer is a liability for you if it is lost. A cash or UPI deposit is cleaner, and our guide to security deposits for bike rental shows how to set the amount.

Red Flags That Mean Stop and Ask More

  • The customer says the licence is "at home" or on another phone, and offers only a photo of it
  • The name, photo or date of birth does not match the second ID
  • They are unwilling to share a mobile number or to take a call from you at the counter
  • They want to rent for many days with a large cash deposit but no ID
  • They are in a hurry and push you to skip the paperwork or the vehicle check
  • The licence class does not match the vehicle they want

One red flag is a reason to ask more questions. Several together are a reason to say no. You are allowed to refuse a rental.

What to Record for Every Rental

Verification is only useful if you can prove later that you did it. For every rental, record:

  1. Customer name and mobile number
  2. Licence number, vehicle class and expiry date
  3. Which second ID you checked, without keeping a copy if you can avoid it
  4. Vehicle number, odometer reading and fuel level at pickup
  5. Damage noted at pickup, with photos
  6. Deposit taken and how it was paid
  7. Pickup and return time

Customer Verification FAQ

Which document is the most important when renting out a vehicle?

The driving licence. It confirms that the person is allowed to drive that class of vehicle. A second ID and a reachable phone number are what let you find them later.

Can I accept a driving licence shown on the phone?

Reports on the MoRTH procedure say traffic officers accept licences shown in DigiLocker or mParivahan as equal to the original. Many rental shops accept them for the same reason, but check that the app loads the record and that the details match the person.

Should I keep a photocopy of the customer's Aadhaar?

UIDAI has advised against sharing Aadhaar photocopies and has said unlicensed private entities should not collect or keep copies. A driving licence plus another ID is the safer option. Ask your CA or lawyer about your own case.

What if a customer has no driving licence?

Do not rent them a vehicle. Without a valid licence for that class, the rental is risky for you and for them.

How long should I keep customer records?

Keep them as long as you may need them for a dispute, an insurance claim or a police query, and store them securely. Ask your CA or lawyer for the period that suits your business.

Quick Verification Checklist

  • Collect Customer Information: Get the customer’s full name, contact number, and address. This helps in maintaining a record and reaching out if needed.
  • Inspect the Vehicle: Always inspect the vehicle together with the customer. Check for any existing damages and note them down to avoid disputes later.
  • Provide a Clear Rental Agreement: Give the customer a rental agreement that outlines all terms and conditions. This ensures transparency and helps avoid misunderstandings.
