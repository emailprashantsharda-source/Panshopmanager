Three questions that cost you money every week

"Deposit kitna tha?"

The customer says ₹1,500. Your notebook says ₹2,000, in handwriting from four days ago. Whoever argues louder wins, and it is usually not you.

"Bas thoda late hua"

Three hours late is overtime you are owed. Nobody at the counter does that maths while a customer is standing there in a hurry.

"Aaj ka kitna bana?"

You cannot tell, because ₹40,000 of deposits is sitting in the same drawer as the rent. Deposit is not income. It is money you owe back.

Before you ask

Fair questions

What if there is no internet at my shop?

You keep working. Rentals and returns are saved on the phone and upload themselves when the signal returns. This is the normal case, not a fallback.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Renting out pauses. Taking bikes back, settling bills and refunding deposits keep working — because bikes already on the road are your customer's problem otherwise, and that is not a fair way to ask for money.

Do I have to type in all my rates?

No. Photograph your printed price list and the app reads the models and rates in. Fix anything it got wrong and you are done.

Can my counter staff see my earnings?

No. Staff can rent out and take back. Money, pricing and settings are owner-only, and every action carries a name and a timestamp.

Is my data only on this one phone?

No. It is on your account and appears on every device you sign in on. Change phones and nothing is lost.
