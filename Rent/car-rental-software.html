How km billing works

For example, say your rate card gives 200 km a day and ₹10 per km after that. A customer takes a car for one day and the odometer shows 320 km driven. The 120 extra kilometres add ₹1,200 to the bill.

The car rental software only adds km charges when the rate card has km terms and the odometer was recorded at both pickup and return — so a missed reading never turns into a wrong bill.

Questions rental shop owners ask

Is this a full car rental management system or just billing?

Both. Bookings, fleet, customer records, km-based billing, deposits and daily earnings are in one app.

Does it track commercial permits?

Yes. Permit, fitness, insurance and PUC expiry dates are tracked per car, and an expired one blocks the car from renting out until it is renewed.

Can I rent out bikes too?

Yes. Cars, bikes and scooties sit in one fleet list. See bike rental software.

What if there is no internet at my shop?

You keep working. Rentals and returns are saved on the phone and upload themselves when the signal returns.

Is my data only on one phone?

No. It is on your account and appears on every device you sign in on. Change phones and nothing is lost.

Can my counter staff see my earnings?

No. Staff can rent out and take back. Money, pricing and settings are owner-only, and every action carries a name and a timestamp.

Do I have to type in all my rates?

No. Photograph your printed price list and the app reads the models and rates in. Fix anything it got wrong and you are done.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Renting out pauses. Taking vehicles back, settling bills and refunding deposits keep working, so no customer on the road is affected.

Do I need to download it from the Play Store?

No. It installs from the browser in seconds, opens full screen like an app and is about 300 KB.
