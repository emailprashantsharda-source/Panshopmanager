What a rental invoice should show

A rental invoice does two jobs. It tells the customer what they are paying for, and it gives you a record for your accounts and for any later dispute. For a car or bike rental, put these on every invoice:

  • Your details: business name, address, phone and GSTIN if you are registered.
  • Invoice number and date: run the numbers in one unbroken series, for example INV-001, INV-002.
  • Customer details: name, phone and driving licence number. For a business customer, add their name, address and GSTIN.
  • Vehicle and period: make, model, registration number, pickup time, return time and odometer readings.
  • Charges, line by line: rent, extra kilometres, late return, fuel, delivery and any damage charge with its bill.
  • Tax: the service code (SAC), the GST rate, and CGST and SGST (or IGST) as separate amounts.
  • Total and payment: invoice total, security deposit held, balance due or refund, and how it was paid.

A GST-registered business must follow the tax invoice rules. Ask your CA to confirm the exact fields for your case, since details such as place of supply matter for business customers.

GST on vehicle rentals

GST on vehicle rentals is not one flat rate for everyone. It depends on whether you rent the vehicle with or without a driver, on the type of vehicle, and on whether you are registered. In August 2025 the Kerala Authority for Advance Ruling held that a self-drive car rental is taxed at the rate that applies to the same type of vehicle sold as goods, not at a flat 18%. An advance ruling binds only the applicant, but it shows that the answer can differ by vehicle.

The template therefore has one editable GST cell. Enter the rate your CA confirms for your service. If your turnover is below the registration limit, you may not need to charge GST at all. For suppliers of services, the usual limit is ₹20 lakh a year (₹10 lakh in some special category states). The limits and rates change, so check the current position before you set up your invoices.

The deposit is not part of the invoice total

A security deposit is money you hold, not money you have earned. Do not add it to the taxable value. Show it as a separate line, so the customer sees the invoice total, the deposit and the balance. If the vehicle comes back damaged, deduct only the repair cost on an actual bill and show it as its own line, as described in our rental vehicle damage policy guide. If you are not registered for GST

If you are not registered, you should not charge or show GST. Issue a simple bill with the same details but without the tax lines. In the template, set the GST rate cell to 0% and leave the GSTIN blank. This keeps the numbering and the records tidy, and it makes the move to GST easier if you register later.

How to use the Excel template

  1. Download the car rental invoice template and open it in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.
  2. Fill the yellow cells: your business, the customer, the vehicle, the pickup and return times.
  3. Enter each charge as quantity × rate and leave unused rows blank.
  4. Set the GST rate to the one your CA confirmed.
  5. Enter the deposit held and check the balance line.
  6. Save one file per invoice and keep invoice numbers in running order, or print the sheet and give it to the customer with the vehicle papers.

Invoice FAQ

Is this invoice format only for cars?

No. The same format works for bikes and scooties. Change the vehicle description, the rate unit (hour or day) and the extra-kilometre rate.

Should the security deposit be included in the invoice amount?

No. Show it as a separate line below the total, as the template does. It is refundable money, not income, until you deduct dues from it.

Do I charge GST on a car rental?

It depends on your registration status, whether a driver is included and the vehicle. Ask your CA which rate applies to your service, then enter it in the template.

What should I do about late returns?

State the late charge in your rental agreement, record the actual return time, and show the late hours as a separate invoice line.

Can I edit the template?

Yes. The yellow cells are inputs and the rest are formulas. You can add your logo and your own terms below the totals.
